Agent skills that read your Obsidian vault instead of learning it twice. Generic skill, your notes stay yours.

## Skills

[`dikology/skills`](https://github.com/dikology/skills) — MIT, [中文 README](https://github.com/dikology/skills/blob/main/README.zh.md).

Each skill assumes an Obsidian vault and nothing else. Your vault keeps one short
section — the vault's [`Overlay`](https://github.com/dikology/skills/blob/main/CONTEXT.md)
— that tells the skill where your index, your log and your rules live, and every field
in it has a stated fallback, so a vault with no Overlay still gets a working session.

## Apps

The apps are what agents helped build.

- [`youtube-cli`](https://github.com/dikology/youtube-cli) — a local-first YouTube CLI
  that gets what you watched into your notes.
- [`chinese-umbrella`](https://github.com/dikology/chinese-umbrella) — a SwiftUI app for
  learning characters through OCR and interactive reading.

More in the pinned row and the repo list.
